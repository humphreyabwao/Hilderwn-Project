Sustainability

Mining responsibly for people and planet

At Hildernw Mining Limited, sustainability is built into every stage of our operations — from extraction to rehabilitation.

Our Approach

Sustainability is not an afterthought

At Hildernw Mining Limited, sustainability is not an afterthought — it is built into every stage of our operations. From responsible land stewardship to clean water management and community investment, we are committed to leaving a positive, lasting legacy.

We believe long-term value comes from mining that respects the environment and uplifts the communities around us. Our integrated approach balances production with protection at every level.

Our Pillars

Three pillars of responsible mining

Environmental Stewardship

Progressive land rehabilitation, biodiversity protection, responsible tailings management, and water recycling at every site we operate.

  • 85% water recycled across all sites
  • Continuous biodiversity monitoring
  • Progressive rehabilitation plans

Community Investment

We invest in the people and places around our operations through employment, education, healthcare, and infrastructure development.

  • $2M+ invested in local communities
  • Skills training and apprenticeships
  • Healthcare and school support

Cleaner Energy

Investing in energy-efficient equipment and lower-emission processing to reduce our operational footprint year on year.

  • Energy-efficient fleet upgrades
  • Lower-emission processing methods
  • 2030 net-zero target
